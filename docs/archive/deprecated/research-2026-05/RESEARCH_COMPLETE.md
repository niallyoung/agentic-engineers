### 3. Model Agnosticism Wins
- **30 frameworks** support multiple LLM providers

- **Flexibility:** Open-source models (Ollama) competing with cloud

### 4. Open-Source Thriving
- **67% of frameworks** remain open-source
- **MIT licensing dominates** (44% of total)
- **Enterprise adoption** growing without moving to commercial

### 5. Enterprise Maturity

- **Durable execution** (Temporal) gaining adoption
